    This chapter is exact theory, computed on a classical computer: with one laser the 302 qubits reduce to a 302 × 302 matrix. The qubit circuits are in chapters 2 and 3.

    Measure it: a spike cascade

    Each step runs a 10-qubit Qiskit circuit on the backend: the neuron that fired and its 9 most connected neighbours. Measuring collapses the superposition onto one neuron, which fires the next step.

    8
    Short τ: measuring that often freezes the walk (Zeno effect). Long τ: the spike travels further.

    The cascade starts from the neuron lit in chapter 1.

      What changes when you measure? Coherent · measured · classical

      Same laser, same τ. The coherent walk is never measured; the measured one is measured every τ on the full network; the cascade is what these 10-qubit circuits do; the classical one is an ordinary random walk. Measuring destroys interference and, with short τ, freezes the walk.
