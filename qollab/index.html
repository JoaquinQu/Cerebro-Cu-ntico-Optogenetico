    2 Quantum spike cascade

    Each step is a 10-qubit Qiskit circuit: the neuron that fired and its 9 neighbours with the most synapses. It runs on the selected backend and is measured; the neuron that comes out as 1 triggers the next step.

    Small τ: the window represents the network better, but measuring that often freezes the walk (Zeno effect). Large τ: the excitation moves further, but leaks out of the window.

    The cascade starts from the neuron illuminated in panel 1.

      What changes when you measure? Coherent · measured · classical

      Same laser and same τ. The coherent walk is never measured; the measured one is measured every τ on the full network; the cascade is what these 10-qubit circuits do; the classical one is an ordinary random walk. Measuring destroys the interference between steps and, with small τ, freezes the walk.
